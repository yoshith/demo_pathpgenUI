MODEL & DATA

What this map can tell you

The default view displays published data. It does not detect all pathogens or provide a validated pathogen forecast.

OBSERVED

NOAA water levels

Battery station 8518750, meters relative to MLLW. UTC timestamps are displayed in New York time. Observations may be preliminary or flagged. The app refreshes every five minutes and marks old or failed updates. The gauge does not measure currents or bacteria.

LAB DATA

NYC DEP bacterial indicators

Enterococci and fecal-coliform results come directly from NYC Open Data's Harbor Water Quality dataset. They are historical lab samples; each point shows its collection date. Refreshing the feed does not make a sample current. Native units and detection-limit qualifiers are retained. Where recent records lack coordinates, an older station location is used and its date is shown. Collection timezone and assay are not specified per result.

SEWERS

Outfall inventory and DEP advisories

Orange markers are official NYS DEC CSO outfall locations. Their presence does not mean they are overflowing. Waterbody areas use DEP's own rainfall/model-based advisory feed: amber indicates a reported advisory, gray indicates no reported advisory, and dashed boundaries indicate unavailable or old status. No advisory does not establish water safety. Discharge rates, source microbial loads, the underground pipe network, and New Jersey outfalls are not supplied by these layers.

sECOM

Stevens numerical model connection

The requested model is Stevens' sECOM. NYHOPS provides an application and forecast interface. No numerical sECOM field is bundled or connected by default. An accessible provider-approved output/feed is needed to drive the arrows. The import accepts prepared geographic east/north velocities; native curvilinear and staggered sECOM output needs a verified conversion first. A model predicts water movement; it does not directly observe pathogens.

SCENARIO

Optional tracer exploration

Select Explore scenario to run a hypothetical release. Without an imported model, its flow is schematic. Tracer colors represent relative mass, not bacterial concentration. Imported files are checked for format, not scientific accuracy. Lab samples and sewer markers do not automatically calibrate or feed the plume.

For a microbial forecast

We need verified hydrodynamics, time-varying source discharge and microbial loading, organism-specific fate parameters, and independent event samples. Streets and basements also require a sewer–surface inundation model. This version has no calibrated concentration field, uncertainty guarantee, or water-safety declaration.

Required file structure

Flow JSON: schemaVersion: 1; kind: hydrodynamic-field; name; crs: EPSG:4326; velocityUnits: m/s; times: UTC ISO timestamps; grid: longitude, latitude, wetMask; u and v: one flat array per time. Longitude changes fastest. See examples/ in the repository.

Samples CSV: id, latitude, longitude, collected_at, target, value, unit, qualifier; optional method. Use =, <, or > as qualifier. Missing values are never treated as zero.
