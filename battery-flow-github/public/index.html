MODEL & DATA

What this map can tell you

This is a working research prototype for one NYC coastal study area. It does not detect all pathogens or provide a validated public-health forecast.

OBSERVED

NOAA water levels

Battery station 8518750, meters relative to MLLW. Times are stored in UTC and displayed in New York time. Observations may be preliminary or flagged. The app refreshes every five minutes and marks old or failed updates.

SCENARIO

Flow arrows and tracer plume

By default, currents are user-set and schematic. They are not inferred from the tide gauge. The plume uses a normalized hypothetical release, dispersion, and a user-set half-life. Colors are relative tracer density, not CFU, gene copies, infection risk, or measured pathogen locations.

IMPORTED

Your model and samples

Import gridded u/v velocities to drive the tracer paths. Imported files are not automatically validated by this app. Laboratory points retain their organism, units, qualifier, method, and collection time; they do not calibrate the plume automatically. Files stay in this browser tab and disappear on reload.

Before making scientific predictions

You need time-varying hydrodynamic fields, appropriate wet/dry masks, measured microbial source loads, organism-specific fate parameters, and independent event samples. Basement and street flooding require a local inundation model. This version has no pluvial inundation, calibrated microbial concentrations, formal uncertainty bounds, or safety declarations.

Required file structure

Flow JSON: schemaVersion: 1; kind: hydrodynamic-field; name; crs: EPSG:4326; velocityUnits: m/s; times: UTC ISO timestamps; grid: longitude, latitude, wetMask; u and v: one flat array per time. Longitude changes fastest. See examples/ in the repository.

Samples CSV: id, latitude, longitude, collected_at, target, value, unit, qualifier; optional method. Use =, <, or > as qualifier. Missing values are never treated as zero.
